# CRM SCOPE (MVP)

## Purpose

Support booking and operations only.
NOT a full CRM system.

## Entities

- Customer
- Traveler
- Booking
- Supplier

## Contact Identity

Each entity may have:
- email
- phone
- zalo
- whatsapp

## Scope

- basic info storage
- link to bookings
- notes

## Out of Scope

- pipelines
- automation
- marketing campaigns

## Future

- optional Odoo integration
- export customer data
