📅 How to subscribe to your Ops Calendar

🍎 Apple Calendar iPhone & Mac
  • Tap 📅 Sync in the ops board top bar to open the panel.
  • Tap 🍎 Apple Calendar — your iPhone/Mac will show a dialog: "Subscribe to Calendar?"
  • Tap Subscribe. Done — the calendar appears in your app within seconds.
  • Or manually: copy the URL, open Calendar app → File → New Calendar Subscription, paste URL → Subscribe.
  • On iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars to manage it later.
🗓 Google Calendar Web & Android
  • Tap 📅 Sync → tap Google Calendar button. A Google page opens.
  • The URL is already pre-filled. Click "Add calendar".
  • Or manually: copy the URL, open Google Calendar → click + next to "Other calendars" → From URL → paste → Add calendar.
  • On Android: the subscribed calendar syncs to the Google Calendar app automatically within a few minutes.
  • Google refreshes every 12–24 hours (this is a Google-side limit, not ours).
📧 Outlook Web & Desktop
  • Tap 📅 Sync → tap Outlook button. Outlook.com opens with the URL pre-filled.
  • Click "Import" or "Subscribe" on the Outlook page.
  • Outlook Desktop (Windows): copy the URL → open Outlook → File → Account Settings → Internet Calendars → New → paste URL → Add.
  • Outlook Desktop (Mac): File → New → Internet Calendar Subscription → paste URL → Subscribe.
📱 Other apps Fantastical · Proton · Notion · Any iCal app
  • Copy the webcal:// URL from the panel.
  • In your calendar app, look for "Add calendar" → "Subscribe from URL" (the exact wording varies by app).
  • Paste the URL and confirm. The calendar will refresh automatically per the app's schedule.
💡 Subscribe once, updates forever. Reminders (1–2 days before + a few hours before each service) are already embedded — no extra setup needed. Share the same URL with your whole team. To revoke access, tap "Regenerate link" in the panel.
