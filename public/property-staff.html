Staff Operations

Front Desk And Operational Workflows Need Their Own Surface.

This workspace is for staff-facing operations, not tenant configuration. Front desk, room status handling, guest support, and future housekeeping or folio flows belong here, separate from the tenant-side property builder.

Tenant config stays in properties-engine: property setup, inventory structure, pricing policy, seasons, and commercial configuration.
Staff operations live here: reservation desk, room-status response, guest support, and operational lanes that should not be mixed into config forms.
