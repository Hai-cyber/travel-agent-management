Common questions

What does "platform commission" mean?

At month-end, we calculate your total confirmed booking volume for that calendar month. If it's under €500, no commission applies. From €500–€1,499 we invoice 1%; €1,500 and above we invoice 2%. This is a separate invoice from us to you — we never deduct anything from your customers' payments.

Do you process customer payments?

No. Tours Market is a software platform. Your customers pay you directly — by bank transfer, cash, or via payment methods you configure. We are not a payment processor or marketplace.

What happens after the 6-month free period?

We will notify you by email before your trial ends. You can subscribe with a card to continue, or your account will move to a limited read-only state. You always retain access to export your data.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings at any time. You keep access until the end of the current billing period. There are no cancellation fees.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer a 14-day refund on your first paid period if you have not published a live storefront or processed any bookings. See our Terms of Service for full details.

What is included in "multi-locale storefront"?

Your Tours Market storefront can serve customers in 11 languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Chinese, Thai, and regional English variants) based on the visitor's browser language or your configured default.

What currency are fees charged in?

Subscription fees are listed in EUR. Your actual charge may vary slightly depending on your card's currency and issuer exchange rate. Commission invoices are also in EUR unless otherwise agreed.

Do I need a custom domain?

Not to start. The Starter tier gives you a free platform subdomain (yourbrand.tours-market.com). The Pro tier unlocks custom domain support once your account is verified.
