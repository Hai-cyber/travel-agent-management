Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 15, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tours Market LLC ("Tours Market", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our software platform at tours-market.com and any subdomain thereof.

Short version: We collect only what we need to operate the platform. We do not sell your personal data. We use industry-standard security. You can request your data or deletion at any time.

1. Who This Applies To

This policy covers two types of people who interact with Tours Market:

  • Operators — travel agencies, tour companies, and hospitality businesses that create a Tours Market account and manage their business through our platform.
  • Visitors — people who visit tours-market.com or access public pages on the platform.

If you are a customer of a travel operator whose storefront is powered by Tours Market, that operator is the data controller for the information you submit to them. Please refer to their privacy policy for how they handle your data.

3. Cookies

Tours Market uses the following cookies:

  • tam_session — An encrypted, HTTP-only session cookie used for authentication. This cookie does not track you across other websites. It expires when you log out or after a fixed idle period.
  • Cloudflare cookies — Our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may set functional cookies for security and CDN purposes. These are operational and do not constitute behavioral advertising.

We do not use advertising, targeting, or third-party tracking cookies. If you use a browser that blocks functional cookies, the platform login and session management will not work correctly.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Tours Market platform
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To process subscription billing, manual membership settlement, or other paid platform services when they are enabled through the selected provider workflow
  • To send you transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, activation notices, and billing notices where applicable)
  • To respond to support requests and account inquiries
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for behavioral advertising. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5. Data We Do Not Control

Customers of your travel business who submit their name, email, or booking details through your Tours Market storefront are providing data to you, as the operator, not to Tours Market. You are the data controller for that information. Tours Market acts as a data processor for that data under your instruction.

You are responsible for maintaining an appropriate privacy notice for your own customers and for handling customer data requests (access, deletion, etc.) that you receive.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only as necessary to operate the platform:

  • Cloudflare — Infrastructure, CDN, Workers runtime, and D1 database hosting. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Stripe or another approved payment provider (when enabled) — When a tenant uses an electronic billing route, the relevant provider processes payment data under its own privacy policy as an independent data controller for regulated payment information.
  • Google (optional) — If you use Google Sign-In, Google authenticates your identity. No further data is shared with Google beyond authentication.
  • Law enforcement / legal — We may disclose data if required by a valid legal obligation or court order.

7. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained for the duration of your subscription.
  • After account termination, we retain minimal records (email, support history, and any billing history if paid services were activated) for up to 3 years for legal and financial compliance.
  • Platform content (tours, booking records) is deleted or anonymized within 90 days of account termination unless you request an export first.
  • Server logs are retained for up to 90 days for security analysis.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / Data Subject Rights)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or other regions with data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — Request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection / Restriction — Object to or request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@tours-market.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Session tokens stored as encrypted, HTTP-only cookies (not accessible to JavaScript)
  • Passwords stored as secure hashes (never in plain text)
  • Asset and media files stored in isolated per-tenant R2 buckets with access controls
  • Database queries parameterized to prevent SQL injection
  • Cloudflare infrastructure with DDoS protection and WAF

No system is completely immune to breach. In the event of a security incident affecting your personal data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. International Data Transfers

Tours Market is operated from infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, which may process data in multiple geographic regions. If you are accessing the platform from the EEA or UK, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where such transfers occur, we rely on Cloudflare's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and applicable transfer mechanisms.

11. Children's Privacy

The Tours Market platform is not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered account holders by email ahead of material changes. The updated policy will be effective from the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
